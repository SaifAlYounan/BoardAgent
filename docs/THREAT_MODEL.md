# BoardAgent threat model

Status: **Gate-2 frozen implementation contract** plus the approved amendments  
Authority: `planning/gate2/gate2/VERIFICATION-AND-RELEASE-NET.md` (TH-01 to TH-63) and
the pinned amendments under `docs/decisions/` (TH-64 to TH-71)  
Scope: BoardAgent v1 beta boundary

## Security posture

BoardAgent is an MCP-native governance system of record. It assumes that clients,
agents, networks, submitted text, operators, and external identity providers may be
malicious or compromised. The deterministic server owns identity, authorization,
canonical records, state transitions, consent evidence, and the audit chain. It does
not trust an agent's interpretation, claim of human review, client brand, or displayed
UI. Intelligence remains at the edge.

Security failures are fail-closed. A denial must not disclose a hidden object's
existence; an audit, evidence, clock, migration, key, or storage fault must never be
silently downgraded. Delivery records prove committed handoff only, never human reading
or comprehension. Indefinite governance retention means there is deliberately no
product purge path.

## Protected assets and trust boundaries

- Person identity, board seats, accountable principals, passkeys, TOTP secrets,
  enrollment invitations, OAuth grants, sessions, refresh families and access JTIs.
- Board existence and membership, documents, submissions, Q&A, votes, proxies,
  ballots, minutes, redlines, signatures, action items, evidence and recusal state.
- Canonical packages, hashes, rulesets, governance profiles, certificates, audit
  events, checkpoints, exports, backups and migration history.
- MCP and OAuth protocol boundaries; auth browser pages; client registration and
  metadata retrieval; database, job, worker, webhook, storage and operator boundaries.

The actors below include anonymous attackers, compromised or negligent MCP clients,
stolen-token holders, invited participants exceeding their authority, secretariat or
management insiders, operators, malicious submitted-content authors, compromised IdPs,
and infrastructure failures. The stated residual risk remains after the required
control; it is not permission to weaken the control.

## Cross-cutting residual-risk allocation

- BoardAgent proves canonical server-side state, delivery, consent context and tamper
  evidence. It does not prove that a human read, understood or safely rendered content.
- Bring-your-own-agent portability means each person is responsible for the security,
  presentation, synchronization, local embeddings or memory and deletion or tombstone
  handling of their agent and copies.
- Machine-readable-only input avoids ambiguous conversion, OCR and binary parser risk.
  Management and secretariat must submit accurate canonical source; BoardAgent rejects
  non-machine-readable material even as evidence and makes no conversion claim.
- Deterministic rules prove reproducibility, not that humans encoded the charter or
  selected a permitted override wisely. Citations, versioning and confirmation preserve
  accountability.
- Indefinite retention strengthens the record but increases breach impact, backup cost
  and data-governance obligations. V1 deliberately has no physical governance purge.
- External timestamp anchoring and independent public-production review are later
  controls. Until T10 passes, the release label cannot exceed private beta.
