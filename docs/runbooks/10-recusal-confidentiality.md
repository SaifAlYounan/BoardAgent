# 10 — Recusal and confidentiality

**Owner:** secretariat/admin under the board's approved process. **Purpose:** apply deny-wins
exclusions across every surface without leaking excluded existence.

1. Identify the exact member, board/matter/resource set, basis, effective time, and whether
   the exclusion is recusal, confidentiality access, or both. Do not infer legal necessity;
   obtain the board's accountable decision.
2. Read current member, object, source package, vote, meeting/minutes, task, feed, export,
   and notification state that may be affected.
3. Use `manage_recusal` or `manage_document_access` with the exact package and complete
   human confirmation.
4. Verify with controlled principals that the excluded identity cannot learn existence,
   count, cursor movement, snippet, pending action, direct-ID result, notification, export,
   certificate detail, or search match. Require the same existence-oblivious result for a
   nonexistent identifier where specified.
5. Verify no active proxy, ballot stage, vote eligibility, minutes/task action, or cached
   draft bypasses the new state. Cancel/restage affected work through supported tools.
6. Verify unaffected entitled principals retain only their intended visibility.

Never test using the excluded person's live credentials without their authorization; use
the regression/synthetic path. Do not explain a hidden object's existence in an error or
operator receipt.

## Evidence

Record before/after policy hashes, confirmation receipt, exact effective
scope, positive/negative checks, and any downstream package replacement required.

## What changes when a person is recused

Use the named record type: board, document, vote, question, meeting or minutes. A meeting
recusal also restricts its agenda, transcript, minutes and source-linked tasks. A minutes
recusal restricts its versions, review/signature records and source-linked tasks. A
question keeps its existing visibility rules, including any source-document restrictions.
A document citation alone does not hide the whole meeting or minutes that cites it.

Each restriction is independent. Lifting a minutes restriction does not override a
remaining meeting or board restriction. The person's appointment and the original records
remain unchanged. A secretary can recuse themselves; another entitled secretary must lift
that restriction. Company administration alone does not override record visibility.

Adding a restriction cancels affected unfinished confirmations and removes affected pending
feed entries. It prevents new notices and actions for the excluded record. Already-valid
minutes signatures remain historical evidence; recusal does not pretend that an unsigned
required signer has signed. Finalization waits for every required signature. Resolve the
conflict or use the documented package correction process; never waive a signer silently.

After lifting, ask the agent to fetch the current record and start a fresh action. Old
confirmation codes and removed feed entries are not restored. For a management question,
the currently entitled assigned owner can give a fresh answer even when their earlier
pending prompt was removed by the recusal; the retained removal receipt is checked.
Check the current question/minutes/task list if the earlier prompt is absent from the
briefing. Board/vote lifts may create fresh vote notices; they never restore old ballots,
proxies or add a seat to an already-frozen electorate.

System-data exports preserve recusal history in their existing authority, meeting and
minutes data classes. Such exports retain their separate confirmed export authority; this
is not an ordinary member reading a hidden record. Backups preserve all canonical history.
