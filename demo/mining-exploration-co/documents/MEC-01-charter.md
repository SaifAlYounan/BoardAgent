# Synthetic board charter

**SYNTHETIC TEST DATA. No real company, person, asset or corporate act is represented.**

Reference: MEC-01-charter. This fictional charter is a test rule source, not a statement
of jurisdictional law.

C1 — Seats. Mira Vale is chair. Mira, Tomas Reed and Leila Stone each have voting weight
one. Rowan Ash is secretary with weight zero and no ordinary administrator powers.
Technical setup authority must not add a fourth voting seat.

C2 — Ordinary decisions. Quorum is at least two thirds of eligible voting weight.
Abstentions count for quorum. Approval requires at least one half of yes/no weight and
every tie is rejected. There is no chair casting vote. Yes/no/abstain fails;
yes/yes/no passes if quorum is met. No votes means no approval.

C3 — Reserved matters. A programme extension above USD 250,000 requires yes votes of at
least two thirds of eligible weight and quorum of at least two thirds. Abstentions count
for quorum and ties fail. The secretary confirms closure after included questions have
recorded answers and source updates are resolved. Ordinary decisions also use confirmed
closure in this trial. The trial notice period is zero seconds; deadlines must be future.

C4 — Proxies. Only another eligible voting member may hold a proxy. A proxy is for one
vote, cannot chain and expires at that vote's deadline. The principal's own later ballot
supersedes the proxy ballot. The non-voting secretary cannot cast or hold a proxy.

C5 — Conflicts. A conflicted member discloses the interest before access or action on
the affected matter. The secretary applies the supported exclusion to that exact matter.
Excluded weight is removed from the eligible denominator. With two remaining eligible
equal-weight members, two thirds requires both to participate and both to vote yes for
a reserved matter. Verify exclusion across reads, search, updates and acts.

C6 — Minutes. Record actual meeting and decision references. Every pending comment or
redline, including one on a superseded version, receives a disposition before signature
readiness. Corrections preserve earlier versions. Signatures bind the exact current
package; a changed package requires fresh signatures.

C7 — Evidence. Close an action only after its owner supplies the required evidence and
the secretary confirms it. A narrative saying work is complete is not a closure event.
Completed actions and final minutes use linked correction cycles.

C8 — Revision. A changed source for an open vote requires source-update disposition and,
when the package is replaced, a new vote with fresh ballots. Do not carry votes, codes
or signatures across packages. Questions and answers retain their exact cut-offs.

C9 — Administration. The setup administrator activates cited profile and ruleset versions
through supported confirmed tools. Rules cannot be inferred from an upload. Secretary
delegation is limited to this board and the actually approved operations. Missing
authority or facts require an explicit decision, not a guessed default.

C10 — Overrides. Rule overrides are forbidden in this trial. A rule change needs a new
approved source and profile/ruleset version. This pack supplies no fabricated server IDs,
signatures, profile hashes or evidence of activation.
